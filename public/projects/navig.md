# NAVIG

- Category: Interactive web
- Status: live
- Year: 2026
- Engagement: Freelance client project
- Role: Sole frontend developer. Owned the frontend architecture and design direction from an abstract product brief.
- Stack: React, Three.js, GLSL, GSAP
- Link: https://navig-app.vercel.app/
- Page: https://www.erhamza.tech/projects/navig

A visually rich website for a crypto and AI intelligence product. As you scroll, a 3D scene of 25,000+ glowing particles reshapes itself into ocean waves, waterfalls and data streams, and it runs at 144 frames per second.

## Highlights

- Cinematic 3D scroll-driven web experience, built around a GPU-driven particle scene
- Procedural shader-based visual states and WebGL blending work

## Results

- 25,000+ GPU-driven particles at 144 FPS (owner-reported)
