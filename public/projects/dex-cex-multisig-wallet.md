Live trading screens for crypto exchanges that show prices as they change, and a multi-signature smart-contract wallet in the style of Safe (formerly Gnosis Safe) that requires approvals from a set minimum number of owners (such as 2 of 3) before any transaction runs. It removes single points of failure for individuals, teams and DAOs managing digital assets on Ethereum and compatible chains.
